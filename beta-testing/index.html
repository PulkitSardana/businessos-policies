Testing BusinessOS Beta

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · हिंदी में पढ़ें

BusinessOS Beta is an early test version of BusinessOS, an Android app for running a small business: customers, invoices, payments and expenses, with voice that works on your phone. It is in a closed test on Google Play, open only to people we invite.

Use made-up data only. Please don't enter real customers, phone numbers, UPI IDs or other real business details. This is a test version: your records exist only on your phone, with no backup.

Who can take part

  • Adults aged 18 or over whom we have invited.
  • An Android phone with Android 8.0 or later. Voice needs Android 13 or later, with on-device speech recognition for your language; without it, you can type instead.
  • Internet access to register and sign in.

How to join

  1. Give us the email address of the Google account you use on Google Play, and the email address you want to register with in the app. They can be the same.
  2. Open the Google Play test link we send you, accept the invitation, and install BusinessOS Beta from Google Play.
  3. Open the app and register with the email address we invited, a password of at least 8 characters and a made-up business name.

By joining the test you agree to the Terms of Use. The Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled.

What you can try

  • Customers, invoices, payments and expenses. Invoices are numbered on your phone, and you can share an invoice as a PDF.
  • A "Cash" account is set up for cash payments when you sign in.
  • Ask BusinessOS: type or say what happened, check what the app prepared, then confirm it. You can also ask simple questions, such as "Who owes me money?".
  • Voice in English, Hindi or Hinglish, recognised on your phone.
  • The app in English or Hindi.
  • Settings › Security & account: sign out of all other phones, or delete your account.

Not in this beta

  • Syncing between phones, and backup.
  • Password reset. If you forget your password, ask us to delete your account (say that you want to stay in the test) and register again.
  • Reversing a payment, voiding an expense, credit notes and invoice corrections, so check before you confirm.
  • Suppliers and bills, stock, and the morning briefing.
  • Gemini understanding: it is not switched on.

Good to know

  • Your records are only on this phone. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage or changing phones deletes them, and there is no backup.
  • Hindi voice understanding is experimental. Check the words and the amount before you confirm.
  • The server can be slow to wake up. After a quiet period, signing in can be slow or fail once. Wait a moment and try again.
  • Things will change. Features can change or be removed, and the test can end at any time.

Feedback and help

Email businessos921@gmail.com. You can also send private feedback from the app's page on Google Play. Please don't include real personal data, or screenshots that show it.

Leaving the test

  1. If you want your account deleted, delete it first, in the app or by email.
  2. Uninstall BusinessOS Beta. This deletes the records on your phone.
  3. To stop receiving test versions, open the Google Play test link again and choose to leave the test.

When the beta ends

The app may no longer be able to sign in. Within 30 days after the beta ends, we delete the server, its database and the invitation list. The records on your phone stay there until you uninstall the app.
