Delete your BusinessOS Beta account

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · हिंदी में पढ़ें

This page explains how to delete your account in BusinessOS Beta, the Android app by BusinessOS App Studio on Google Play, and what happens to your data. You can delete your account in the app, or ask us by email without the app.

Ask by email (no app needed)

Send the request from the email address you registered with:

Email a deletion request

Or write to businessos921@gmail.com with the subject Delete my BusinessOS account.

In the app

  1. Open BusinessOS Beta and sign in. You need an internet connection.
  2. Tap Settings, then Security & account.
  3. Under Delete account, enter your password and tap Delete my account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted from our server straight away, and the app removes its data from your phone. If you have forgotten your password, ask by email: password reset isn't available in this beta.

By email: how it works

  1. Email us from the address you registered with, using the button above.
  2. To make sure the request is really yours, we reply to your account's email address and ask you to confirm. Reply to that email to confirm.
  3. We delete your account within 7 days after you confirm, and email you when it's done.

We will never ask for your password, a sign-in code or any other secret. Don't send them to us.

If you write from a different address, we send the confirmation only to your account's address. If you can no longer receive email at that address, delete the account in the app instead.

What is deleted

From our server

  • your account: your email address and password hash;
  • your organisation, its default business profile and its "Cash" account;
  • every business of which you are the only member, with all its records on the server (a business shared with other members stays for them, and only your membership is removed);
  • your sign-ins: no phone stays signed in to your account.

If you ask by email, we also remove your address from the invitation list, unless you tell us you want to stay in the test. After you delete your account in the app, your address stays on the invitation list so that you can register again; email us if you want it removed.

From your phone

  • Deleting in the app also removes from that phone your business records (customers, invoices, payments, expenses), your Gemini choice, your sign-in and the invoice PDFs the app made for sharing.
  • Copies you have already shared or saved elsewhere are not affected. App settings such as language stay until you uninstall the app.
  • An email request can't reach your phone. To remove the records there, uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings › Apps › BusinessOS Beta › Storage.

What is kept, and for how long

WhatHow long
Deleted server records in Neon's database recovery historyUp to 6 hours, then they are gone
Server logs (they contain no email addresses or passwords)7 days
Request-limit counters (IP address, account ID, email hash)Up to 1 hour
Our emails about your requestUntil 30 days after the beta ends, as a record that we handled it
Your place on the Google Play tester listUntil you leave the test or we remove you. Google handles this list.

Nothing else is kept: we make no other backups. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

Your business records are only on your phone. To remove them but keep your account, clear the app's storage, then sign in again. To withdraw your agreement to Gemini understanding, use Settings › Language & voice. For anything else, email businessos921@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
